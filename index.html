      // how unlocking works
      
Scan, pay, verified — done

      
Payments happen through a UPI QR code. Your money goes straight from your UPI app; the site never asks for card details or wallet logins.

    

    

      
Pick a Pro language
Open it in the IDE and press Unlock. A payment QR appears with the exact amount pre-filled.

      
Scan with any UPI app
GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM — scan and pay. The payee details live inside the QR, so no numbers float around as plain text.

      
Submit your UTR
Every UPI receipt has a 12-digit UTR / transaction ID. Paste it in the form under the QR.

      
Access activates
The payment is verified against bank records and your plan switches on. Every step is written to a tamper-evident hash-chained ledger.

    

  







  

    

      // questions
      
Fair questions, straight answers

    

    

      

        Do C, C++ and Java really compile, or is it fake?
        
Really compile. Code is sent to an open execution service (Piston) that runs real GCC and the JDK, then the output — including compiler errors — comes back to your screen. You need internet for these languages.

      

      

        Can my programs take input, like scanf and cin?
        
Yes. There is a Program Input box under the editor — whatever you type there is fed to your program as standard input, exactly like typing into a terminal.

      

      

        What is the hash-chained ledger? Does it hide my payment?
        
No — and be careful of anyone who claims otherwise. UPI payments always have a bank trail; that is what makes them safe. The ledger does something different: every payment event is chained with SHA-256 hashes, so if any record is edited later, verification fails. It proves records weren't tampered with. What stays private is simpler — the owner's number is never displayed on the site; it lives only inside the QR.

      

      

        Why do I have to submit a UTR number?
        
The UTR (a 12-digit ID on every UPI receipt) is how your payment is matched to your account and verified against the bank statement before your plan activates. It usually takes a few hours at most.

      

      

        Is my code saved?
        
Your code auto-saves in your browser per language, and you can download any file with one click — handy for lab record submissions.
